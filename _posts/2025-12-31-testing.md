# Training AI Co-Scientists Using Rubric Rewards

**Link:** [arXiv:2512.23707](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.23707)

Researchers proposed a solution to **open-ended** research problems.

---


## Rubric-Guided Reinforcement Learning
### 1. Data Mining from "The Collective Corpus of Science"
The authors built an automated pipeline to extract training data from existing scientific papers.
*   **Input:** A scientific paper.
*   **Extraction:** A model extracts the **Research Goal** (the problem the authors faced) and **Goal-Specific Rubrics** (the constraints and requirements the solution had to meet).
*   **Dataset:** `ResearchPlanGen`, covering Machine Learning, Medical, and ArXiv papers.

### 2. The "Generator-Verifier Gap"
The core innovation is how they reward the model during training.
*   **The Task:** The policy model (Generator) sees only the *Research Goal* and must write a plan.
*   **The Grader:** A frozen copy of the model acts as the Judge. The Judge is given **Privileged Information**: it sees the *Rubrics* extracted from the original paper.
*   **The Result:** Because the Judge has the "answer key" (the rubrics), it can accurately grade the Generator, even if the Generator is the same size model. This allows for self-improvement via RL.
